01 / The idea

Close what you meant to. Keep what matters.

A single panel in your menu bar shows what's running, groups it by what's safe to close, and leaves the decision to you.

⊘
01

Review before you close

A clear preview separates your apps, verified background processes, protected system services, and your exclusions.

⇲
02

Graceful first, force last

Apps are asked to quit normally. Only verified, user-owned items that ignore the request are force-closed — and only if you allow it.

⛨
03

Conservative by default

Anything TaskZero can't confidently identify is left untouched. Unknown means protected, never eligible.

⟲
04

Stop any time

Cancel a cleanup mid-run. Remaining items are skipped and no new signals are sent after you stop.

◱
05

Read-only monitor

A live process monitor for inspection only — it never terminates anything. Right-click to add an exclusion.

⎇
06

Exclusions & history

Permanently skip VPNs, sync clients, or audio tools. Every cleanup is summarized and kept locally.

02 / Safety model

It would rather do nothing than do harm.

Every candidate passes a layered, default-deny check. Identity is re-validated again right before any force action.

  • ✓
    Your own apps & verified processes

    Current-user GUI apps and Developer-ID / ad-hoc signed background processes are eligible.

  • ✕
    System & Apple background services

    launchd, WindowServer, Finder, Dock and friends are always protected.

  • ✕
    Other users & root-owned processes

    Anything not owned by you is never targeted.

  • ✕
    Unknown or unverifiable identities

    If the signature can't be confirmed, it stays protected.

  • ✕
    Recycled PIDs

    Executable path, start time, and signature are re-checked before any signal — a reused PID is skipped.

  • ✕
    No privilege escalation

    Never uses sudo, never changes SIP or Gatekeeper, never unloads launch agents or daemons.

03 / Built with

Native, local-first, and private.

No account, no analytics, no telemetry, no network calls. Inspection and cleanup happen entirely on your Mac.

04 / On your Mac

A tidy Mac, one click away.

1

Download & unzip

Open the ZIP to reveal TaskZero.app and drag it to Applications.

2

Approve it

On first launch, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

3

Click the glyph

TaskZero lives in the menu bar. Click it, review, and clean.
